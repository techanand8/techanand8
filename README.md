## 💡 ABOUT ME

I am an **Electronics and Communication Engineering (ECE)** graduate who is **passionately curious** about how computer chips work from the inside out. 

* 🔍 **Primary Focus:** **Design Verification (DV)** — Deep diving into UVM testbenches, writing SVA (SystemVerilog Assertions), and using constrained-random stimulus to verify complex digital logic.
* ⚡ **Hardware Design:** Writing clean, synthesizable **SystemVerilog & Verilog RTL** for datapaths, FSM controllers, and synchronous logic.
* 🚀 **Future Horizon:** Exploring **Hardware Acceleration** — understanding how intense compute and AI algorithms are accelerated on silicon.
* 💻 **My Setup:** I tuned my laptop environment as **MOS (Mayank OS)** — a custom Linux workstation configured with fast terminal tools and automation scripts to streamline simulation and verification.
